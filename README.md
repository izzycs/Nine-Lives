# NINE LIVES

A top-down horror chase through a haunted house. You are a cat, something huge is loose in the house, and you have to survive from 3:00 AM until dawn (about 3 minutes).

Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` (HTML + CSS + JavaScript, no libraries, images, audio files, build step or server). Open it in Chrome to play, or on a phone or tablet in landscape.

## Modes

- **Solo:** you are the cat; the computer hunts you.
- **Two players (one keyboard, split screen):** cat vs dog.

## Controls

| Action | Solo | 2P cat | 2P dog |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrows / WASD | WASD | Arrows |
| Sprint / charge | Shift | Left Shift | Right Shift |
| Dash / pounce | Space | Space | `/` or Enter |

P / Esc pause · M mute · F FPS counter · R restart

When the dog catches you, type the key sequence shown to struggle free. In solo, one wrong key ends the night, and every struggle you win makes the next one longer and faster (up to five keys). In 2-player, both players race to finish their own sequence.

### Touch (phones and tablets)

Hold the device sideways. Touch controls appear automatically on touch screens (and the moment you tap the screen on a hybrid device; pressing a key hides them again).

| Action | Touch |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen (a stick appears under your thumb) |
| Sprint | Hold the SPRINT button |
| Dash | Tap the DASH button |
| Struggle free | Tap the arrow pad that appears (or swipe anywhere) in each direction shown |
| Pause, sound, fullscreen, restart, menu | Pause button at the bottom centre |

Two-player mode needs a keyboard. On Android, FULLSCREEN also locks the screen to landscape; on iPhone, add the page to your home screen for a full-screen view.

## Tips

- Solo is meant to be brutal. The dog outruns a walking cat from the start and a sprinting one by the end of the night, cuts you off instead of following your tail, and pounces from further away.
- Hide under beds and tables, but don't stay put while the dog is close. In solo its jaws reach under the edge of the furniture, your nerve breaks fast, and after a panic you're too scared to crawl back under anything for a few seconds.
- In solo, losing it completely only buys you a few seconds: the lights flicker, the growl stops, and it steps out of the dark right behind you. Keep moving and keep looking back.
- Vents are cat-only shortcuts, not hiding places. Your nerve bleeds while you're inside, after a few seconds the metal groans loud enough for the whole house to hear, and when you climb out the grates stay shut for seven seconds.
- Smash a vase right next to the dog to stun it. The crash is loud.
- Use headphones: the growl and paw steps tell you which side the dog is on.
